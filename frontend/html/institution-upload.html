How It Works

Build your institution's private question bank

  1. Upload question papers — PDF, DOCX, or TXT files containing MCQs
  2. AI extracts questions — Our system parses and extracts individual MCQs automatically
  3. Questions are scoped to your institution — Only your students can access them
  4. Create exams — Go to the Exams tab to generate exam sets from your question bank
Note: You need at least 80 questions per subject to create an exam (4 sets × 20 questions).
